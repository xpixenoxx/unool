## Executive Summary

An independent, code-to-database forensic investigation of **Unool Studio** (`unool.co`, operated by Pixenox Solutions) establishes that the current Settings surface (`app/dashboard/settings/`) cannot simply be reskinned or reorganized along the lines of the prior transformation blueprint. Live Postgres inspection paired with end-to-end route tracing uncovered **critical authentication bypasses, broken GDPR erasure queries, cross-workspace leakage, live RLS flaws, and phantom developer/notification promises** that were either missed or misclassified in prior documentation.

### Core Diagnosis

1. **Product Reality vs. Settings Surface**: Unool is a creator and brand growth workspace combining a **Public Profile Builder** (`/dashboard/profile`), **Multi-Platform Content Composer & Scheduler** (`/dashboard/composer`, `/dashboard/schedule`), **Studio AI Personalization** (`/dashboard/studio`), **SignalDM / Comment Automation** (`/dashboard/automation`), **Follow Gates** (`/dashboard/follow-gates`), **FlightPlan**, and **Cashfree Subscription Billing** (`/dashboard/billing`). However, the existing 9-tab Settings page (`SettingsClient.tsx`) behaves like an unstructured dumping ground containing:
   - Duplicate/orphaned tabs (`Templates`, `Template Analytics`, and legacy `/dashboard/settings/billing/BillingClient.tsx`),
   - Unimplemented developer illusions (`IntegrationsTab` hitting a non-existent `api_keys` table in production and saving webhooks exclusively to browser `localStorage`),
   - Hardcoded placeholder or static-UI switches (`NotificationsTab` static UI prefs, unused `mfaEnabled` state in `SettingsClient.tsx`), and
   - Direct links broken by target mismatch (`?tab=persona` x3 and `?tab=billing` x4).
2. **Critical Security & Data-Integrity Blockers**:
   - **P0 Auth Bypass on `/api/auth/*`**: `middleware.ts` (`L10`, `L209-211`) returns early for `publicPaths` (`'/api/auth'`) **before** stripping `x-user-id` and `x-workspace-id` at `L241-244`, while `/api/auth/switch-account`, `/api/auth/me`, `/api/auth/status`, and `/api/auth/platform/connect` trust those headers in `getCurrentAuth()` / header checks. Spoofing `x-user-id` against `GET /api/auth/switch-account` mints a valid 30-day HMAC switch token for any user UUID.
   - **P0 Multi-Workspace `.single()` Multiplication**: `lib/auth/context.ts` (`L139-167`), `middleware.ts` (`L267-276`), and related helpers use `.single()` on `workspace_members`. Any user belonging to `>1` workspace causes `.single()` to fail with `null`, either setting `x-workspace-id = user.id` or inserting a brand-new ghost `'Personal Workspace'` (`plan: 'free'`) on every SSR page render.
   - **P0 Broken GDPR/DPDP Erasure & PII Orphan**: Both the live Postgres function `public.delete_workspace_data` and `app/api/user/delete/route.ts` (`L178`) query `referrals` with non-existent column `referrer_id` (live DB column is `referrer_user_id`), causing the atomic RPC to fail inside `try/catch`, and `DELETE /api/user/delete` never deletes `public.users` (which has no FK to `auth.users` — child tables cascade from `public.users`), leaving orphaned PII rows after `auth.admin.deleteUser()`.
   - **P0/P1 Live Supabase RLS Tautologies & Asymmetric Policies**: Live `pg_policies` inspection shows `admin_users` and `admin_audit_log` use self-referential `{public}` `SELECT` tautologies (`id IN (SELECT id FROM admin_users)`) without checking `auth.uid()`, `analytics_events` permits `{public}` `INSERT WITH CHECK (true)`, and `workspace_members` `SELECT` restricts reads to `workspaces.owner_id = auth.uid()`—breaking user-scoped queries for invited non-owner members.
3. **Prior Blueprint Corrections**: The earlier `SETTINGS_COMPLETE_PRODUCT_INVESTIGATION_AND_TRANSFORMATION_BLUEPRINT.md` contradicts itself on navigation count (`"7-section"` at `L191`, 9 listed items at `L200-241`, `"8 clean items + Danger Zone"` at `L243`), retains a non-functional `Developers` tab despite zero `/api/v1/*` consumers and missing `api_keys` table in production, and recommends Phase 2 notification toggles (`In-app alerts`, `Weekly Digest`, `AI Suggestions Ready`) for background workers that do not exist in the codebase (`vercel.json` has only `instagram-sync` and `publish-scheduled`).

## Part 1: Unool Product Architecture & Settings' Role (`PART 1`)

### 1.1 What Unool Is Built to Do
Established directly from `app/dashboard/`, `app/api/`, `app/privacy/page.tsx`, `app/terms/page.tsx`, and live Postgres schema:
- **Core Capabilities**:
  1. **Public Profile & Presence** (`profiles`, `/dashboard/profile`, `/u/[subdomain]`): Customizable link-in-bio / portfolio page supporting 24–25 themes, custom domain DNS verification (`_unool-challenge`), lead capture (`profile_leads`), viewer allowlisting (`profile_viewers`), and 30-day analytics.
  2. **Multi-Platform Publishing & Scheduling** (`posts`, `post_variants`, `platform_posts`, `platform_connections`, `/dashboard/composer`, `/dashboard/schedule`, `/dashboard/publish`): Single-idea authoring adapted by AI for 19+ supported channels (Instagram, Facebook, Threads, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Pinterest, Reddit, Discord, Bluesky, Mastodon, Lemmy, Listmonk, MeWe, Dev.to, Hashnode, Medium, Kick, Telegram, etc.), multi-account per platform (`20261114000001_multi_account_platform_connections.sql`), and QStash-backed scheduled dispatch.
  3. **Studio AI Personalization** (`profiles.studio_profile`, `lib/ai/studioContext.ts`): Captures 7 persona attributes (`role`, `businessStage`, `businessDomain`/`doing`, `audience`, `tone`, `style`, `interests`) sanitized against prompt injection (`sanitizeProfileValue`) to shape Composer drafts, Studio Ideas, and FlightPlan outputs.
  4. **Engagement Automation & Follow Gates** (`instagram_*`, `facebook_automation_config`, `threads_automation_config`, `follow_gates`, `/dashboard/automation`, `/dashboard/follow-gates`): Auto-reply/DM concierge workflows and follow-to-unlock gates.
  5. **Monetization & Plans** (`plans`, `subscriptions`, `payment_orders`, `lib/billing/`): Cashfree PG payment orders in INR/USD for `spark` (Free), `creator`, `pro`, and `studio` tiers (note live `workspaces.plan` DB default remains legacy `'free'`).

### 1.2 Account & Workspace Model
- **Users (`auth.users` + `public.users`)**: Every signup creates an `auth.users` row via `supabaseAdmin.auth.admin.createUser`, triggering `public.handle_new_user()` (`ON_AUTH_USER_CREATED`) which inserts into `public.users` (`id`, `email`, `full_name`), creates a default workspace (`'My Workspace'`, `plan: 'free'`), adds `workspace_members (role: 'owner')`, and provisions a default `profiles` row.
- **Workspaces (`workspaces` + `workspace_members`)**: Content, connected accounts, automation rules, and profiles belong conceptually to a `workspace_id`. Multi-account quick switching in the browser is stored in `localStorage` (`unool_saved_accounts_v1`) containing signed HMAC `switchToken` payloads for up to 8 accounts (`app/dashboard/layout.tsx:84-233`).
- **Admin Layer (`admin_users`, `admin_audit_log`)**: Separate admin portal (`/admin/settings`, `/api/admin/impersonate`) allows `super_admin`, `admin`, and `support` roles to manage plans, overrides, feature flags, and impersonate users via `httpOnly` cookies `impersonating` / `impersonated_as` (`/api/auth/impersonate/callback/route.ts`).

### 1.3 What Truly Belongs in Settings vs. Contextual Modules
Settings must serve as **the calm, reliable control plane for account identity, security, notification consent, connected channels, workspace governance, AI voice defaults, and subscription status**—NOT a redundant mirror of contextual builders:
- **Belongs in Settings**: Personal preferences (name sync, timezone/locale), Authentication & Security (password, TOTP 2FA, sessions, saved-device switchers), Email Notifications & Privacy Controls (data export, AI processing opt-out, consent notices, personal account erasure), Connected Social Channels & health/reconnects, Shared AI Voice / Brand Persona defaults, Workspace Name & Team Members / Ownership / Plan summary (linking to `/dashboard/billing` for checkout UI).
- **Belongs Elsewhere (Contextual)**: Profile visual theme & bio/links/custom domain customization (`/dashboard/profile`), per-post adaptation & publishing toggles (`/dashboard/composer`), DM/comment keyword & velocity automation rules (`/dashboard/automation`), Cashfree checkout modals (`/dashboard/billing`).

## Additional Discoveries Beyond the Existing Settings Blueprint

> **Mandatory `PART 1` Independent Verification**: Every discovery below was directly verified in source files or live Postgres (`bhkpkylcwcxtpxbxrpag`) metadata in this investigation and either contradicts, corrects, or extends `SETTINGS_COMPLETE_PRODUCT_INVESTIGATION_AND_TRANSFORMATION_BLUEPRINT.md` (`Blueprint v1`) and `SETTINGS_COMPLETE_AUDIT_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_PROMPT.md`.

### Discovery 1: P0 Pre-Strip Header Auth Bypass in `middleware.ts` on `/api/auth/*`
- **Evidence**: `middleware.ts:10` includes `'/api/auth'` in `publicPaths`; `middleware.ts:209-211` returns early (`if (!hostSubdomain && isPublicPath(pathname)) return refResponse ?? NextResponse.next()`) **before** header stripping at `middleware.ts:241-244` (`sanitizedHeaders.delete('x-user-id')`). Meanwhile `lib/auth/server.ts:19-34`, `app/api/auth/switch-account/route.ts:18`, `app/api/auth/status/route.ts:10`, and `app/api/auth/platform/connect/route.ts:46` trust `x-user-id` / `x-workspace-id`. Furthermore, `lib/utils/__tests__/forensicAuditFixes.test.ts:14` only tests `/api/profile`, completely missing `/api/auth/*`.
- **Impact**: An unauthenticated attacker passing `x-user-id: <victim_uuid>` to `GET /api/auth/switch-account` receives a validly signed 30-day HMAC `switchToken`, then submits `POST /api/auth/switch-account` to obtain an active session for the victim account.

### Discovery 2: P0/P1 Multi-Workspace `.single()` Crash & Ghost `'Personal Workspace'` Multiplication
- **Evidence**: `lib/auth/context.ts:139-167` runs `.from('workspace_members').select('workspace_id').eq('user_id', user.id).single()`, and when `membership` is falsy (which PostgREST returns whenever row count is `0` **or** `>1`), `L145-164` executes `workspaces.insert({ owner_id: user.id, name: 'Personal Workspace', plan: 'free' })`. Similarly, `middleware.ts:267-276` uses `.single()` and falls back to `workspaceId = result.data?.workspace_id || user.id`.
- **Impact**: Once a user owns or is invited to 2+ workspaces, every SSR render inserts yet another `'Personal Workspace'` row or injects their `user.id` as a bogus `x-workspace-id`. Also creates `'free'` instead of `'spark'`.

### Discovery 3: P0 Live `delete_workspace_data` RPC & `/api/user/delete` Broken Column + PII Orphan
- **Evidence (Live Postgres `bhkpkylcwcxtpxbxrpag`)**:
  - Live `public.referrals` columns: `id`, `referrer_user_id`, `referred_user_id`, `referral_code`, `status`, `reward_type`, `reward_applied_at`, `created_at`.
  - Live `pg_proc` for `public.delete_workspace_data` (`20261111000001_complete_account_deletion_and_audit_fixes.sql:87`) executes `DELETE FROM public.referrals WHERE referrer_id = target_user_id OR ...` (`referrer_id` does not exist!). `app/api/user/delete/route.ts:178` repeats `{ table: 'referrals', column: 'referrer_id' }`.
  - Live FK inspection shows `workspaces.owner_id`, `workspace_members.user_id`, `profiles.user_id`, `platform_connections.user_id`, `profile_viewers.user_id`, `onboarding_checklists.user_id` reference `public.users`, while `public.users` has **zero FK referencing `auth.users`**. Neither `delete_workspace_data` nor `DELETE /api/user/delete` deletes `public.users`, leaving `email` and `full_name` orphaned after `auth.admin.deleteUser()`.
  - Live `pg_tables` shows `api_keys`, `media_uploads`, and `deleted_accounts` **do not exist** in production `public`. Meanwhile `signup/verify-otp/route.ts:95,107` and `delete/route.ts:46,142,146` query those missing tables.

### Discovery 4: P0/P1 Live Supabase RLS Policy Flaws (`pg_policies`)
- **Evidence (Live `pg_policies` on `bhkpkylcwcxtpxbxrpag`)**:
  - `admin_users` (`"Admins can view admin_users"`) & `admin_audit_log` (`"Admins can view audit log"`): `roles = {public}`, `cmd = SELECT`, `qual = "(id IN ( SELECT admin_users_1.id FROM admin_users admin_users_1))"` — a self-referential tautology with **no `auth.uid()` or email check**, permitting anon/authenticated REST callers using `SUPABASE_ANON_KEY` to read admin table rows (note: schema includes `email, role, permissions, password_hash, is_active`).
  - `analytics_events`: `"Service role can insert analytics"` uses `roles = {public}`, `cmd = INSERT`, `with_check = "true"`, allowing arbitrary unauthenticated `INSERT`s via Supabase client REST API.
  - `workspace_members`: SELECT policy `"Members can view workspace members"` checks ONLY `workspaces.owner_id = auth.uid()`. Because `workspaces` SELECT policy `"Members can view workspace"` subqueries `workspace_members`, **invited non-owner members querying via user-scoped RLS receive 0 `workspace_members` rows**!
  - Cross-table mismatch: `platform_connections`, `subscriptions`, `payment_orders` use `user_id = auth.uid()` RLS while backend features operate on `workspace_id`.

### Discovery 5: P1 Unauthenticated Header/Body User Spoofing in Analytics & Unvalidated Request Endpoint
- **Evidence**:
  - `app/api/analytics/time-spent/route.ts:68-78`: If session auth is missing, reads arbitrary `request.headers.get('x-user-id') || bodyUserId`, confirms user existence with `supabaseAdmin.auth.admin.getUserById(candidateUserId)`, and mutates `user_section_time`.
  - `app/api/platform/request/route.ts:14-44`: Catches missing auth silently, sets `effectiveWorkspaceId = workspaceId || 'default'`, tries inserting string `'default'` into `uuid NOT NULL` column `analytics_events.workspace_id`, catches DB error quietly, and still returns `200 { success: true, ticketId }`.
  - `app/api/platform/kick/stream-settings/route.ts:18-45`: Allows any signed-in user to pass arbitrary `body.workspaceId` without `verifyWorkspaceAccess()` and overwrite encrypted RTMP stream credentials; `.single()` breaks on multi-account workspaces.

### Discovery 6: P1 Authentication Dual Password-Hash Split & Non-Revocable 30-Day `switchToken`
- **Evidence**: `app/api/auth/signup/verify-otp/route.ts:119,193-195,217` stores the hashed password inside `auth.users.raw_user_meta_data.password_hash` while scrambling Supabase Auth's native password to random bytes so JWT sessions remain alive.
- **Consequence**: `user_metadata` is embedded in client-accessible session objects and by default modifiable via `supabase.auth.updateUser({ data })` unless restricted. Meanwhile `lib/auth/account-switch-token.ts` issues 30-day `{ uid, email, iat, exp }` tokens with **no `security_version` or password-change epoch**; resetting password does not revoke existing switch tokens in `unool_saved_accounts_v1`, and `handleSignOut` (`layout.tsx:435-443`, `SettingsClient.tsx`, `/api/auth/logout`, `/api/auth/signout`) neither purges `unool_saved_accounts_v1` nor clears `impersonating` / `impersonated_as` cookies.

### Discovery 7: P1 Cross-Workspace Platform Connection Bleeding & Production Debug Leakage
- **Evidence**: `app/api/platform/connections/route.ts:163-189` (Strategy 3) falls back to returning connections from *other* workspaces if the active workspace has none, AND `L348` returns `debug: string[]` in every response. Yet `DELETE /api/platform/connections` (`L377`) strictly enforces `conn.workspaceId === auth.workspaceId`, causing `404 Connection not found` when a user tries to disconnect an account displayed via Strategy 3.

### Discovery 8: P1 Prior Blueprint Contradictions, Dead Developer Illusion & Fictional Notifications
- **Blueprint Contradictions**: `SETTINGS_COMPLETE_PRODUCT_INVESTIGATION_AND_TRANSFORMATION_BLUEPRINT.md` states `"7-section"` (`L191`), enumerates 9 sections (`L200-241`), then concludes `"8 clean items + Danger Zone"` (`L243`).
