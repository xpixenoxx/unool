## 1. Executive Summary

Unool Studio is a creator and brand operating system built by **Pixenox Solutions** that combines two core pillars: (1) an AI-assisted multi-platform social publishing, scheduling, engagement automation, and growth suite; and (2) a customizable multi-template public profile / link-in-bio + lead-capture micro-site builder. Across a product of that breadth, **Settings should act as the trusted control tower** for identity, workspace governance, social channel health, AI voice calibration, notifications, billing/usage clarity, security, developer integrations, and compliant data lifecycle controls.

Instead, forensic code inspection paired with read-only inspection of the live production Supabase database (`bhkpkylcwcxtpxbxrpag`) reveals that `/dashboard/settings` is structurally fragmented, commercially under-leveraged, visually split across two conflicting design systems, and **actively broken or deceptive across several core workflows**:

- **Broken / Non-Persistent Controls Presented as Saved (`P0`/`P1`)**:
  - `NotificationsTab` saves all 4 notification toggles exclusively to browser `localStorage` (`unool_notification_prefs`) with zero backend API or email/digest integration (`components/dashboard/settings/tabs/NotificationsTab.tsx:12-76`).
  - `IntegrationsTab` saves webhook URLs exclusively to `localStorage` (`unool_webhook_endpoints`) (`IntegrationsTab.tsx:59-110`) with zero backend persistence or outbound delivery.
  - `POST /api/keys` writes to an `api_keys` table that **does not exist in live Supabase** (`bhkpkylcwcxtpxbxrpag`), enforces a tautological SQL RLS policy in migration (`20250123000001_create_api_keys_table.sql:39-70`), strips `'webhooks:manage'` offered by the UI, silently drops `expiresAt` (`expiresInDays` mismatch), stores an identical `'uk_live_'` prefix for every key (`SupabaseApiKeyRepository.ts:38`), and exports `validateApiKey` / `requireApiKey` in `lib/auth/api-key.ts` with **zero callers anywhere in `app/api/`**.
- **Critical Multi-Tenant & Security Vulnerabilities (`P0`)**:
  - `DELETE /api/user/delete` unconditionally includes the user's active `auth.workspaceId` in `ownedWorkspaceIds` even when the caller is only a non-owner member (`app/api/user/delete/route.ts:29-34, 157-171`), enabling a team member deleting their own personal account to wipe all posts, profiles, and connections of another owner's workspace while leaving the workspace shell intact. It also targets the wrong R2 bucket env var (`CF_R2_BUCKET_NAME` instead of `CF_R2_MEDIA_BUCKET_NAME` / `CF_R2_AVATARS_BUCKET_NAME`), uses a wrong `referrals.referrer_id` column in its JS fallback (`L178`), omits Cashfree subscription cancellation and OAuth token revocation, and purges `payment_orders` and `subscriptions` despite a 7-year Indian tax-record commitment in `/privacy` and `/terms`.
  - All **15 custom-token platform connect routes** under `/api/auth/platform/*/connect/route.ts` sit inside `middleware.ts`'s `publicPaths` (`/api/auth`) and trust client-supplied `body.workspaceId` **without calling `verifyWorkspaceAccess` or checking `planCtx.limits.channels`**.
  - `GET/POST/DELETE /api/onboarding` trusts `workspaceId` from query/body without `verifyWorkspaceAccess`.
  - `PUT /api/workspace` allows any member to rename a workspace without owner/admin RBAC; `POST /api/workspace` allows inviting arbitrary roles without RBAC and leaves `// TODO: Send invitation email` unimplemented (`app/api/workspace/route.ts:34-64, 114`).
  - `POST /api/auth/switch-account` bypasses `mfa_enabled` (`app/api/auth/switch-account/route.ts:60-177`); `POST /api/auth/reset-password/request` does not block deleted/suspended users and `verify-and-update` does not revoke existing sessions.
  - `lib/auth/server.ts:75-100` throws `'Auth resolution failed'` instead of returning `null`, causing unauthenticated requests to routes like `DELETE /api/user/delete` (which calls `getCurrentAuth` outside `try/catch`) to return HTTP `500` instead of `401`.
- **Disconnected Navigation & Cross-App Dead Ends (`P1`)**:
  - `SettingsClient.tsx` calls `useSearchParams()` (`L66`) yet **never reads `?tab=`**, hardcoding `useState<SectionId>('workspace')`. Every inbound deep link across the app — `/dashboard/settings?tab=persona` (`ComposerClient.tsx:1827, 2043`, `StudioIdeaGenerator.tsx:394`) and `/dashboard/settings?tab=billing` (`domain/route.ts:61`, `profile/route.ts:128, 142`, `enforcer.ts:48`) — lands on the wrong tab or a tab that does not exist.
  - Email token-expiry alerts (`lib/auth/email.ts:157`) link users to `/dashboard/channels`, a route that **does not exist** (`app/dashboard/channels` absent from repo).
  - `/privacy` (`app/privacy/page.tsx:507`) directs users to `Dashboard > Settings > Connected Platforms` to disconnect social accounts — even though Settings has **no social platforms tab** (connections live inside a modal launched from `DashboardClient.tsx`).
  - `app/dashboard/layout.tsx` swaps descriptions for `Studio` and `Signal` (`L358, L372`), still refers to `'Presence'` (`L458`) despite `/dashboard/presence` redirecting to `/dashboard/profile`, and leaks hardcoded `'usha'` / `'usha@unool.co'` fallback strings (`L695, L742, L827`, also `ComposerClient.tsx:1705`).
- **Missing Table-Stakes SaaS & Commercial Features (`P1`/`P2`)**:
  - No editable personal Account Profile tab (name, avatar, email display, timezone), no active sessions management (static `"coming in v1.1"` text in `SecurityTab.tsx:103-107`), no authenticated change-password UI, no team member management UI (despite `workspace_members` schema & plan seat limits), no self-service GDPR/DPDP data export (promised in `/privacy` Section 10), no workspace usage metering UI, and no GST tax profile / downloadable invoices (promised in `/terms`).
  - Two confusing low-value tabs (`Templates` and `Profile Metrics` / `analytics`) clutter the 8-tab Settings sidebar despite full template customization and profile analytics living inside `/dashboard/profile`.

**Headline Verdict:** Unool's Settings module should **not** be patched piecemeal. It must be re-architected around clear **Personal vs. Workspace scope separation**, harmonized into the **Cream/Terracotta/Espresso editorial design system**, hardened with **strict multi-tenant RBAC & live database schema alignment**, connected to **real Channel Health, Billing/Usage, Notifications, Team, and Privacy Lifecycle workflows**, and cleaned of all fake/client-only illusions.

## 5. User-Perspective Evaluation & Friction Analysis

A Settings page can compile cleanly and still destroy user trust. Evaluated across 8 real-world user journeys:

1. **First-Time / Non-Technical User ("I just signed up — where do I connect Instagram or set my profile picture?")**:
   - Opens `Settings` expecting personal profile settings (display name, avatar, connected social channels, plan details). Instead lands on **Workspace** with a generic `Workspace Name` input, a cryptic UUID (`Workspace ID`), and an outdated `"Free"` badge.
   - Zero guidance on where to connect social channels (even though Compliance docs & 4 feature CTAs send them to Settings to connect accounts!) or edit their name/avatar (`users.full_name` / `avatar_url`).
2. **Busy Professional Changing One Setting Quickly ("I clicked 'Update Brand Voice' in Composer to tweak my tone")**:
   - Clicks `/dashboard/settings?tab=persona` -> lands on `Workspace` tab because `?tab=` is ignored! Must hunt through 8 tabs to guess that `"Studio AI"` holds Brand Voice/Persona, losing scroll context and deep-link bookmarking.
   - When switching tabs with unsaved edits in `Workspace` or `Studio AI`, no dirty-state warning fires — edits silently vanish when unmounted (`SettingsClient.tsx:354-465` conditional render).
3. **User Experiencing an AI-Powered App for the First Time**:
   - `StudioTab` exposes raw developer schema strings (`workInterests`, `Primary Hat / Role`, `Voice Tone Cadence`), doesn't explain *which tools* read these fields (`Ideas`, `Composer Adapt`, `Week Arc`) vs which don't (`Humanizer`, `Hook Score`), and saves silent defaults (`'Software/AI'`, `'Creator'`) if fields are cleared (`StudioTab.tsx:114-127`).
4. **User Recovering from a Mistake / Network Failure**:
   - In `WorkspaceTab`, if `PUT /api/workspace` returns `500` or `401`, `SettingsClient` pops `toast.error('Failed to update workspace')` AND `WorkspaceTab.tsx:29` immediately pops `toast.success('Workspace name updated')` — leaving the user seeing red error and green success simultaneously! Meanwhile, saving an empty Studio interest field silently overwrites with `'General topics'`.
5. **Returning User Expecting Preferences to Persist Across Devices**:
   - Enables `'Publish Failed'` and `'Weekly Digest'` in `Notifications`, sees `"Preferences saved"`, logs in from phone or another browser -> toggles reset to `true/true/true/false` defaults because state only lived in `localStorage`. Same false promise occurs when adding a webhook endpoint (`unool_webhook_endpoints`).
6. **User on a Mobile Device (`390px` viewport)**:
   - While `SettingsClient.tsx:284` turns the sidebar into a horizontal overflow bar (`overflow-x-auto`), `LoadingSkeleton` (`shared.tsx:177-186`) hardcodes `flex gap-6` with `w-52` left column even at `390px`, squeezing skeleton cards.
   - `WorkspaceTab.tsx:68` fixes Workspace Name input to `w-44` (`176px`), `IntegrationsTab.tsx:517` uses `w-56` webhook input beside `Save`/`Cancel` buttons inside `SectionCard`'s `action` slot causing horizontal crowding on narrow screens.
   - Horizontal tab strip lacks scroll-fade affordance or dropdown fallback for 8 tabs.
7. **Keyboard & Screen-Reader / Accessibility User**:
   - `SettingsClient.tsx:323` attaches **`onFocus={() => setActiveSection(section.id)}`** alongside `ArrowUp/ArrowDown`. Simply pressing `Tab` into the tablist triggers automatic activation and unmounts/remounts panels unpredictably; worse, `aria-controls` targets `panel-${section.id}`, which does not exist in DOM for inactive tabs (`activeSection === ... &&`), violating WAI-ARIA `tablist` patterns when unrendered panels are referenced.
   - `SecurityTab.tsx:71-80` builds a custom toggle `<button role="switch" aria-checked={mfaEnabled} aria-pressed={mfaEnabled}>` missing `type="button"`, missing `aria-label`/`aria-labelledby` (meaning screen readers read an unlabeled switch!), combining conflicting `aria-checked` + `aria-pressed`, and suppressing focus indicators (`focus-visible:outline-none`).
   - `WorkspaceTab.tsx:90-93` (Copy UUID) and `IntegrationsTab.tsx:344-355, 459-466` (Copy/Delete key) omit `aria-label` on icon-only buttons; `DangerZoneTab.tsx:57` renders raw `"You&apos;ll"` in a JS string prop!
8. **Security- & Privacy-Conscious User**:
   - Sees `"Active Sessions: Full per-device session management is coming in v1.1"` -> zero way to verify active logins or sign out stolen devices.
   - Wants to change password without logging out -> impossible (only Forgot Password OTP exists on `/signin`).
   - Wants to download GDPR/DPDP data before closing account -> impossible, despite `/privacy` Section 10 promising self-service portability.
   - Sees `"Danger Zone"` warning `"There is no recovery path"` vs Privacy Policy Section 7 claiming *"permanently deleted within 30 days"*.

## 7. Recommended Settings Information Architecture & Rationale

### Design Principles

1. **Scope Clarity First (Personal vs. Workspace)**: Follow proven SaaS IA (Linear, Notion, Vercel) by separating **Account** (affects *only you* across every workspace: your name, login security, notification delivery, data export) from **Workspace** (affects the shared brand/team space: workspace name, connected social channels, AI voice signature, team members, billing & developer webhooks/API).
2. **Maximum Clarity, Minimum Nesting (7 Focused Sections, Zero Dead Stubs)**: Replace the current 8 mismatched tabs (`Workspace`, `Studio AI`, `Integrations`, `Templates` [stub], `Profile Metrics` [stub], `Notifications` [fake], `Security`, `Danger Zone`) with a logical **2-group, 7-section flat navigation** that directly covers all active SaaS capabilities without sub-submenus.
3. **Configuration vs. Contextual Work**: Keep **Profile Builder design/templates/links/custom-domain** in `/dashboard/profile` (`TemplatesTab` & `TemplateAnalyticsTab` removed from Settings with `?tab=` redirects), while keeping **Billing Checkout** on `/dashboard/billing` linked bidirectionally with a real **Billing & Usage** summary tab in Settings.
4. **Canonical URLs & 100% Backward-Compatible Redirect Map**: Every `?tab=<key>` syncs cleanly to URL search params, and every broken link across the codebase (`?tab=persona`, `?tab=billing`, `/dashboard/channels`) resolves automatically.

2. **Soft-Delete Recovery vs Self-Service Hard Purge Drift**:
   - `/admin/users/[id]` (`DELETE`) archives soft-deleted users into `deleted_accounts` (`SupabaseAdminRepository.ts:1632, 1871`), surfaced under `/admin/recovery`. However: (a) **`deleted_accounts` does NOT exist in live production DB** (`bhkpkylcwcxtpxbxrpag`), and (b) user-triggered `/api/user/delete` bypasses `deleted_accounts` entirely and hard-purges immediately, creating a contradiction with `/privacy` § 7 (*"within 30 days"*).
   - **Fix**: Deploy a unified account-deletion & recovery policy (either 30-day scheduled purge using `deleted_accounts` with proper RLS & automated cron purge after 30 days, OR immediate hard purge with updated `/privacy` wording + instant cancellation). Surface pending deletion date & admin recovery audit trail accurately in `/admin/recovery`.
3. **DSAR (Privacy Request) & Support Tooling**:
   - Privacy Policy § 10 commits Unool to acknowledging GDPR/DPDP requests within **48 hours** and resolving within **15 working days**. Giving users self-service **Data Export** (`GET /api/user/export`) and **Leave/Delete** inside Settings removes 90% of manual support tickets; admins should also gain an audit-logged `"Generate DSAR Export"` & `"Inspect User Workspace Membership"` tool inside `/admin/users/[id]` and `/admin/workspaces/[id]` (showing whether `user_id` is `owner` vs `member`, connected channel status without tokens, and billing/override records).
4. **Admin Impersonation Hygiene**:
   - `app/api/admin/impersonate/route.ts` / `/api/auth/impersonate/callback` allows admin impersonation; Settings must display a non-dismissible `"Viewing as <user> (Admin Impersonation)"` banner and **block destructive actions (`Delete Account`, `Change Password`, `Disable MFA`, `Modify Keys`) during impersonated admin sessions** unless explicitly permitted/logged.
